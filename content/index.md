Architecture decisions, platform patterns, and operational notes. These are working notes: written to be useful to future me and to colleagues, kept short, and corrected when they turn out to be wrong.

> [!info] Scope
> The notes lean towards Kubernetes, GitOps and Google Cloud platform engineering. Each note says which versions or products it was checked against, and carries a `modified` date so you can judge how fresh it is.

Press `/` to search every note, or browse by section in the index below, by topic on the [[tags|tags page]], or with the explorer on the left. Each note lists its backlinks and a local graph on the right; the global graph shows how the whole set connects.

Notes are plain Markdown with YAML frontmatter, linked with `[[wikilinks]]`, and live in the `content/` folder of a Git repository. Decisions are recorded as ADRs and never rewritten; they are superseded by a new ADR instead. See [[standards/writing-notes|Writing notes]] for the details.
