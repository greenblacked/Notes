Working notes, written to be useful to future me and to colleagues, kept short and corrected when they turn out to be wrong. They lean towards Kubernetes, GitOps and Google Cloud platform engineering, and the engineering-manager side of running a DevOps team, and each one says which versions or products it was checked against and carries a `modified` date, so you can judge how fresh it is.

Start from a section below, press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) to search every note, or open **Browse** in the top bar for the full tree. Topics are on the [[tags|tags page]]. Notes are plain Markdown with YAML frontmatter, linked with `[[wikilinks]]`, and live in the `content/` folder of a Git repository. Decisions are recorded as ADRs and never rewritten; they are superseded by a new ADR instead. See [[standards/writing-notes|Writing notes]] for the details.
